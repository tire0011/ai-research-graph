# Connect humans and agents

**Question:** Can relevant humans/agents be notified and coordinate work across teams and systems?

**Success test:** Show a consented, auditable cross-team handoff and whether participants were already coordinating.

| Platform | Evidence status | Source-backed observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI-on-Demand (AIoD)](https://www.aiodp.ai/) | adjacent | Ecosystem mapping and RAIL support research collaboration; no cross-platform autonomous agent trigger protocol verified. [Official source](https://www.iais.fraunhofer.de/en/press-events/press-releases/AI-on-Demand_Platform.html) |
| [EOSC EU Node](https://open-science-cloud.ec.europa.eu/) | adjacent | Federated collaboration, shared services and access are documented, not automatic human-agent or agent-agent matching. [Official source](https://open-science-cloud.ec.europa.eu/support/getting-started-eosc-eu-node) |
| [AI4EOSC](https://ai4eosc.eu/) | adjacent | AI-module composition and common APIs exist, but the documented module infrastructure alone is not an agent-to-agent conversation network. [Official source](https://docs.ai4eosc.eu/en/latest/getting-started/overview.html) |
| [EOSC-ARENA](https://arena.ai4eosc.eu/) | planned | Work package 6 explicitly plans composable agents and an agent-to-agent interoperability protocol; deliverables are still in preparation. [Official source](https://arena.ai4eosc.eu/project-deliverables/) |
