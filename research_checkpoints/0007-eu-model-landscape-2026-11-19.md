# Checkpoint 0007 — European AI collaboration landscape (2026-11-19)

**Original question:** Why do European AI developers often not know about each
other's initiatives? Could the existing research graph map and *test* useful
connections, using the freshly released OVHcloud Olala model as a probe?

**Scope:** The existing evidence graph is retained. A 25-entry, source-qualified
model-project registry and additive offline overlay were added as a **pilot**,
not as a claim that Europe lacks platforms or that this graph outperforms them.
Existing discovery catalogues and AIoD must remain the retrieval baseline.

**Proven evidence:** Public documents describe HPLT/OpenEuroLLM joint releases
and publisher-reported shared Jean Zay training by Olala and Luciole. These are
two different relationship types: already-known cooperation versus
shared infrastructure (not proof of coordination). Three architecture/evaluation
handoffs are hypotheses with falsifiers, not graph facts.

**Unknown:** Whether teams already coordinate privately, whether models have
compatible inference/test contracts, whether a concrete task needs this,
whether the catalogue is current, or whether graph-assisted review is better
than direct source reading.

**Smallest next experiment:** For Olala↔Luciole, collect matching current
benchmark and model-card evidence plus one concrete maintainer decision.
Independently compare graph-path and ordinary search using identical sources.
Stop if no actionable, source-backed decision emerges. Preserve a negative
result, do not scale based on attractive visual connections.

**Project mission unchanged:** Detect duplicated research, identify
low-effort/high-impact interventions, enable credible cooperation and
mechanistic cross-disciplinary hypothesis discovery. Additional nodes are not
a success metric.
