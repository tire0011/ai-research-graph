# Historical 2023-cutoff pilot — later-evidence audit (2024–2026)

**Outcome inspected:** 2026-11-15
**Frozen retrospective hypothesis seeds:** [HYPOTHESES.md](HYPOTHESES.md), committed **before the follow-up search below**.
**Source rule:** only the four published 2023 papers in the registered baseline may motivate the initial hypotheses. Works published in 2024–2026 are outcome checks, not pre-cutoff inputs.

## Most important limitation

**This is NOT a genuine forecast.** The 2026 assistant has knowledge of later work, and the 2024 *Pinch of Multilinguality* publication had already appeared in an initial mixed-date search before the hypotheses were committed (clearly declared in the hypotheses file). Even a close match with that paper is **not evidence** that the graph can predict future discoveries. The hypotheses were **not generated by our graph** or by an independently time-blind model. This is a *methodological shake-down* of dated source/proposal/outcome separation.

## What the source comparison actually teaches

1. **Plausible ideas are easy to generate after the fact.** H23-01 appears impressive but the outcome was already partly known to the model. Treat this as a leakage **failure**, not a positive forecasting result.
2. **Predictions must be conjunctions of testable claims.** A paper supporting "small language dose helps" does not prove "small dose AND at most 2pp English loss AND under matched budget." Mark precise clauses unverified.
3. **Mechanism and performance need different evidence.** A geometric or influence correlation does not imply a causal explanation. H23-03 is a candidate *future experiment*, not a retrospective discovery.
4. **A graph can earn its place as an auditable research memory** by preserving dated observations, hypotheses, contrasts and counterevidence. That benefit still needs to be compared with an ordinary source-linked research notebook or search, not assumed.
5. **Stop overbuilding retrieval infrastructure:** the existing graph vs text retrieval studies show equal exact accuracy; our three-case source-parity check finds no distinct edge evidence. The next value test should judge multi-source *decision trace and prediction quality*, not link-count or lookup latency.

## Next viable research design (genuinely harder)

A forecasting study needs a training-cutoff-consistent model *and* time-locked retrieval/metadata, or a prospective benchmark where predictions are publicly registered **before** new research outcomes exist. Independently authored baselines and blind external evaluation are important. Prefer a smaller **prospective hypothesis registry** for genuinely unanswered 2026 questions over further unblinded "predictions of 2024".

A practical, low-compute next candidate is H23-03 reformulated prospectively: compare a predetermined cheap **data-influence or gradient-agreement statistic** with static similarity and a simple baseline for predicting *direction* of unseen-language task transfer, on a small multilingual checkpoint. Before coding, perform a stronger 2024–2026 prior-art sweep to ensure this specific comparison is not already done. This file does not claim it is novel.
